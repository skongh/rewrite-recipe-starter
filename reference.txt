Official reference: https://gradle.org/

Homepage summary:
Gradle's homepage presents it as a high-performance build automation platform for JVM and native projects, emphasizing fast incremental builds, a rich plugin and dependency ecosystem, and tight integrations with IDEs and CI/CD pipelines. It spotlights Build Scan diagnostics, Kotlin-based configuration, enterprise support, and learning resources that help teams automate, collaborate, and deliver software quickly.
