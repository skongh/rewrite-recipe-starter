Homepage summary:
The Gradle homepage positions Gradle Build Tool as a way to accelerate developer productivity through fast, incremental builds, deep dependency insights, and automation for JVM, Android, and multi-language projects. It highlights the Kotlin DSL, rich plugin ecosystem, and Build Scan diagnostics, alongside Develocity enterprise capabilities, migration guides from Maven and Ant, IDE integrations, learning resources like DPE University, and adoption stories from companies such as Netflix.
